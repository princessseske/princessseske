# Princess Sarah Maikyau

MSc Business Analytics graduate from Warwick Business School, University of Warwick, with a BSc in Accounting and audit and assurance experience.

I use Python, SQL and R to analyse financial and operational data, evaluate models and translate findings into business recommendations. My interests include data and business analysis, commercial performance and credit risk.

## Selected projects

- **Retail supply chain SQL:** investigate waste, stockouts and supplier performance using relational data and defined business metrics.
- **Generator rental optimisation:** allocate limited capacity across rental durations and examine the revenue and utilisation trade-offs.
- **Credit risk analytics:** compare historical lending policies, segment borrowers and evaluate default prediction.
- **Banking application prediction:** evaluate classification models and the precision-recall trade-off for customer targeting.
- **Financial volatility forecasting:** model time-varying market volatility and explore illustrative option-pricing implications.

These projects are being prepared for publication. Project repositories and verified results will be linked here as they become available.

## Background

My audit experience includes financial-statement testing, reconciliation and internal-control review for energy and utilities clients. I bring that attention to evidence and accuracy to my analytical work.
